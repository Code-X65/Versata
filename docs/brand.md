Versata Digital Solutions — Brand Guidelines

1. Brand Overview

Brand name: Versata Digital Solutions

Brand positioning:
A modern digital-solutions organization focused on technology, innovation, business transformation, and practical digital services.

Brand personality:

Professional

Modern

Innovative

Trustworthy

Precise

Forward-looking

Technology-driven

Accessible

The visual identity should communicate digital innovation, movement, growth, reliability, and progress.

2. Logo

The primary logo consists of:

A stylized teal/blue ribbon monogram.

A flowing V/D inspired symbol.

An upward-right arrow representing growth and progress.

The versatadigitalsolutions wordmark.

Logo Rules

Preserve the original proportions of the logo.

Do not stretch, skew, rotate, or distort the logo.

Do not change the logo's core colors.

Do not add unnecessary shadows, outlines, or effects.

Maintain sufficient clear space around the logo.

Use the standalone symbol when a compact mark or favicon is required.

Use the full wordmark when sufficient horizontal space is available.

Logo Backgrounds

Preferred:

White

Very light neutral backgrounds

Dark navy backgrounds when using an appropriate light/bright logo treatment

Avoid:

Busy photographs directly behind the logo

Low-contrast backgrounds

Colors that visually compete with the teal/blue mark

4. Brand Gradient

The primary brand gradient should visually connect the teal and blue portions of the logo.

Primary Gradient

linear-gradient(135deg, #00AFA9 0%, #1557B0 100%)

Extended Gradient

linear-gradient(120deg, #00AFA9 0%, #168FC0 45%, #1557B0 100%)

Gradient Usage

Use gradients selectively for:

Hero visual accents

Primary CTA emphasis

Decorative shapes

Icon backgrounds

Highlighted text

Technology-themed visual elements

Subtle borders or glow effects

Do not apply gradients to every component. The gradient should remain a recognizable brand accent.

5. Color Usage Ratio

A recommended visual balance for the website:

60% — White / light neutral backgrounds

25% — Navy / dark typography and structural elements

10% — Blue

5% — Teal / cyan accents

The goal is a clean professional interface with controlled use of the stronger brand colors.

The typography should be modern, clean, and highly readable.

Recommended Font Direction

Use a modern sans-serif family such as:

Plus Jakarta Sans

DM Sans

Typography Hierarchy

H1

Bold

H2

Strong visual hierarchy

H3

Comfortable line height

High readability

10. Border Radius

Recommended:

Small controls: 8px
Buttons: 10–12px
Cards: 16px
Large feature panels: 20–24px

Use consistent rounding throughout the interface.

11. Shadows

Shadows should be subtle.

Light UI

box-shadow: 0 8px 30px rgba(16, 42, 86, 0.08);

Elevated Component

box-shadow: 0 15px 40px rgba(16, 42, 86, 0.12);

Avoid excessive shadows or glossy effects.

12. Iconography

Icons should be:

Clean

Minimal

Geometric

Consistent in stroke width

Easy to recognize

Recommended visual direction:

Line icons

Rounded stroke ends

Teal/blue accent treatment

Navy for neutral interface icons

Icons should support the content rather than dominate it.

13. Photography & Imagery

Photography should communicate:

Technology

Digital transformation

Business

Innovation

Collaboration

African business environments where relevant

Modern workplaces

Real-world technology applications

Avoid generic, overly staged stock imagery whenever possible.

Images should have clean compositions and sufficient negative space for text overlays.

14. Website Visual Direction

The website should feel:

Modern + Professional + Digital + Trustworthy + Forward-looking

Preferred Layout

Generous whitespace

Strong typography

Clear content hierarchy

Clean navigation

Large hero sections

Structured service cards

Subtle motion

Controlled gradients

Strong CTA hierarchy

Avoid:

Excessive gradients

Excessive animations

Cluttered layouts

Excessive glassmorphism

Heavy 3D effects

Neon cyberpunk styling

Too many competing colors

15. Motion & Animation

Animations should communicate quality and progression.

Recommended:

Smooth fade-ins

Subtle slide-up reveals

Hover elevation

Gradient transitions

Gentle logo/shape movement

Scroll-based section reveals

Subtle background motion

Animations should be fast enough to feel responsive and subtle enough not to distract from the content.

16. Brand Voice

Communication should be:

Clear

Confident

Professional

Helpful

Practical

Technology-aware

Business-focused

Avoid:

Excessive technical jargon

Overpromising

Generic marketing clichés

Aggressive sales language

Unnecessary complexity

Voice Example

Prefer:

"We help businesses use technology to simplify operations, improve efficiency, and build for growth."

Instead of:

"We revolutionize the digital landscape with groundbreaking next-generation technological innovations."

The brand should sound knowledgeable without sounding exaggerated.

18. Brand Principle

The central visual principle of Versata Digital Solutions is:

Technology that moves businesses forward.

Every website design decision should reinforce the ideas of digital transformation, progress, reliability, and growth while preserving the clean teal-and-blue identity of the existing logo.
